# Trust vs. Reputation

Trust and reputation are related, but they are not the same.

**Trust is personal.**

It is built through repeated experiences and relationships.

**Reputation is portable.**

It allows people who don't already know you to make informed decisions about working with you.

Professional event planners already solve trust through their private vendor networks.

The challenge is that this trust rarely extends beyond those networks.

A talented vendor may be trusted by one planner while remaining invisible to every other planner or client.

## How Koordi Distinguishes Trust and Reputation

| | Trust | Reputation |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Built by | Repeated collaboration | Verified documentation |
| Lives in | A planner's private network | A vendor's public profile |
| Transferable | No | Yes |
| Scalable | No | Yes |
| What Koordi does | Does not replace it | Makes it portable |

Trust remains personal.

Reputation makes trust discoverable.

Koordi does not replace the relationships planners have built. It makes the reputation vendors have earned visible to everyone.
