# Vendor Discovery: Discovered With Trust

When we studied how event organizers find vendors, we learned that experienced planners rarely perform "open searches" for new talent. They rely on established, private networks. Trust is built through past relationships and direct referrals.

At first, we concluded that since organizers aren't actively searching for unknown vendors, a platform focused on discovery wouldn't work.

But looking at the ecosystem from the vendor's perspective revealed a different story.

## The Vendor's Problem: Trapped Reputation

Vendors face a severe limitation: their reputation is often trapped inside a single planner's private network.

A talented vendor may be highly trusted by one organizer, but remain completely invisible to every other planner or client in the city. They cannot easily transfer the trust they've earned in one relationship to create new opportunities in another.

## Discovery Is Not Enough

We quickly realized a crucial truth: **discovery without trust is useless.**

Even if a planner discovers a new vendor, they will not hire them if they cannot ascertain their reliability. In the event industry, the stakes are too high to take a chance on an unverified stranger.

Therefore, the value proposition cannot just be "get discovered." It must be **"get discovered with trust."**

## How Koordi Delivers Discovery With Trust

| What Vendors Get | What Planners See |
|:---|:---|
| A verified profile with a documented Reliability Score | A vendor with a proven track record, not just a portfolio |
| A Pioneer badge that signals they passed verification | A signal that this vendor was among the first to be vetted |
| Priority placement in searches | The most reliable vendors appear first |

## The Entry Point

Koordi enters the market by providing vendors with discovery powered by a verified history of their past work. Planners don't just see a vendor's profile; they see the trust that vendor has already earned.

This solves an immediate problem for vendors while providing planners with reliable talent outside their immediate network.

Once this "discovery with trust" is operational, the rest of the system—escrow, dispute resolution, coordination tools—can be built on top of it.
