# The Event Organizer Workflow & The Limits of Private Networks

To understand how a portable reputation system can actually add value in event commerce, we mapped out what event organizers do all day.

We found that their workflow relies heavily on private networks and informal trust. Here is a high-level map of the typical professional organizer's workflow, and where those private networks begin to show their limitations.

## 1. Concept & Contracting
- **Client Discovery:** Understanding the client's vision and budget.
- **Contracting:** Finalizing agreements. The client is essentially hiring the planner's access to a trusted network.
- *Tools used:* Word docs, email, PDF signers.

## 2. The Sourcing Phase (The Trust Bottleneck)
- **Vendor Activation:** Reaching out to their trusted network to check availability for dates.
- **The Limitation:** If their primary trusted vendors are booked, the planner must expand their search. This is incredibly painful because there is no standardized way to verify the reputation of a vendor outside their immediate circle. They resort to calling peers and asking for favors.
- *Tools used:* WhatsApp, phone calls.

## 3. The Coordination Phase
- **Timeline Creation:** Building the "Run of Show."
- **Logistics Alignment:** Ensuring every vendor knows their load-in time and deliverables.
- **The Limitation:** Because trust is established informally, coordination is handled informally. Important details are scattered across text messages and emails, leading to miscommunications that threaten the success of the event.

## 4. Execution (Day of Event)
- **Firefighting:** Managing the reality of the event.
- **Vendor Management:** Directing traffic and ensuring the timeline is followed.

## 5. Post-Event & Reconciliation
- **Final Payments & Debriefing:** Noting which vendors overperformed.
- **The Limitation:** The vendor earns trust, but that trust is trapped in the planner's head. It does not become a portable asset for the vendor to use to win future business elsewhere.

## Where Koordi Fits

| Workflow Limitation | Koordi Feature |
|:---|:---|
| No way to verify a vendor outside the planner's network | **Verification:** A three-part process that documents ID, portfolio, references, and backup plans |
| Trust is trapped in the planner's head | **Reliability Score:** A public, data-driven score that travels with the vendor |
| Informal coordination leads to miscommunication | **Planner Dashboard:** A single interface for finding, comparing, and booking vendors |
| Vendor reputation is invisible outside the network | **Pioneer Badge:** A visible credential that signals the vendor has passed verification |

Technology cannot replace the human relationships built during execution. But it can address the limitations of the Sourcing and Post-Event phases. By capturing the successful completion of an event, we build a verified history for the vendor. Over time, this portable reputation system removes the friction during the Sourcing phase, allowing planners to confidently hire outside their immediate private network when necessary.
