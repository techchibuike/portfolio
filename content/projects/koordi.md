# Koordi

## The Context

The event ecosystem is built on relationships between organizers, planners, vendors, and clients. At first glance, it appears that trust is the central challenge—organizers worry about unreliable vendors, while vendors worry about delayed payments and inconsistent opportunities.

We investigated this assumption.

## What We Found

Professional event planners rarely depend on unknown vendors.

Over years of executing events, they build private networks of photographers, caterers, decorators, MCs, rental companies, security teams, and other service providers they have personally worked with.

Each successful event strengthens these relationships.

Each failed experience removes someone from the network.

Trust, in this part of the market, is not created by technology.

It is created through repeated collaboration.

These private networks already solve the trust challenge for experienced planners.

## The Gap

These networks are effective, but they are limited.

They remain:
- Personal
- Relationship-driven
- Difficult to scale
- Difficult to transfer
- Invisible outside existing relationships

A vendor's reputation is often trapped inside the planner's network.

A planner's years of relationship-building cannot easily be shared, expanded, or preserved.

The system works remarkably well—but only within the boundaries of the people who built it.

## What Koordi Builds

Koordi is building the public equivalent of what private networks already provide:

- **Open** instead of closed
- **Structured** instead of personal
- **Portable** instead of non-transferable
- **Scalable** instead of capped

Koordi does not replace private relationships. It makes reputation portable, visible, and scalable.

## What Is Real Today

Vendor verification is open now.

Koordi verifies vendors through:
- Government ID confirmation
- Portfolio and social media audit
- Live video proof of workspace and equipment
- Three recent client references
- A documented backup plan

Vendors who pass verification become Pioneers—the first cohort of verified vendors in the network.

## What Is Coming

The full system launches with the app:

- **Escrow:** Payments secured before the event, released after delivery
- **Reliability Score:** A public, data-driven score calculated from arrival time, delivery quality, and communication responsiveness
- **Planner Dashboard:** A tool for planners to find, compare, and book verified vendors

Verification is the foundation. The app is the infrastructure. Both are being built now.

## Where We Are Today

Koordi is not a research project. It is a system under construction.

Verification is live. The first Pioneers are being onboarded. The app is being built in parallel.

The launch will happen when the network is ready—not before.
