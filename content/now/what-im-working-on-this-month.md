# What I'm Working On

*Last updated: July 2026*

This month, I am executing the Phase A playbook for Koordi.

We have validated our core assumptions. We know that private networks work but do not scale. We know that vendors need portable reputation. We know that planners need a way to hire outside their network without starting from zero.

Now we are building.

## Current Priorities

1. **Vendor Verification:** Onboarding the first 50 Pioneer vendors. Verification is open now, and we are processing applications in 5–7 days.

2. **Community Building:** The Koordi Early Access Community is live. We are bringing vendors in a few at a time, gathering feedback, and building the trust that will make the reveal land when it happens.

3. **App Development:** The app is being built in parallel. The three required features—vendor profiles, booking/escrow trace, and the planner dashboard—are being developed for launch.

4. **Documenting the Process:** I am writing down everything we are learning so that the playbook can be replicated when we scale.

We are not researching anymore. We are executing.
